About the Game

It's all about moving like a ghost through the city

What starts as basic delivery work becomes this whole strategic puzzle.
Players must navigate through guarded areas, avoid detection, and choose the safest path.
Every level's like a little puzzle - it's not about being fast, it's about paying attention.

How to Play

Study the Area

Observe patrol routes, lights, and obstacles before moving.

Choose Your Path

Find safe routes across rooftops, streets, and hidden passages.

Move Carefully

Time your actions to avoid being seen or heard.

Deliver the Package

Reach the destination without triggering alarms.

Escape Cleanly

Complete the mission and move to the next delivery.

Featurese

This isn't one of those games where you just mash buttons and hope for the best.
If you're the type who likes to think before you act, you'll love this.

  • Stealth-based mechanics

  • Urban night environments

  • Patrol and detection systems

  • Gradual increase in complexity

  • Multiple paths per each level

Level Design

Each level is designed as a compact environment with multiple solutions.
Players can experiment with different approaches and improve their routes. You'll keep coming back to perfect your results.
